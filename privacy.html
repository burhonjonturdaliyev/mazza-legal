Mazza mobil ilovasi · Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 30-oktabr

Ushbu siyosat “Mazza Trips” MCHJ (STIR 312912170, manzil: Andijon viloyati, Asaka tumani, Yuqori Markayuz ko‘chasi, 122-uy) tomonidan taqdim etiladigan Mazza mobil ilovasi va mazzajoy.uz xizmati foydalanuvchilarning shaxsiy ma’lumotlarini qanday yig‘ishi, ishlatishi, saqlashi va himoya qilishini tushuntiradi. Ilovadan foydalanish orqali siz ushbu siyosatga rozilik bildirasiz.

1. Qanday ma’lumotlarni yig‘amiz

  • Akkaunt ma’lumotlari: telefon raqami (SMS orqali kirish uchun), ism, ixtiyoriy ravishda email va profil rasmi.
  • Bron ma’lumotlari: tanlangan mulk (dacha, mehmonxona va h.k.), sanalar, kirish/chiqish vaqti, mehmonlar soni, to‘lov holati.
  • Hamkor (agent) ma’lumotlari: e’lon qilingan mulk haqidagi ma’lumotlar, rasmlar, manzil va geolokatsiya, shartnoma hujjatlari, mablag‘ yechib olish uchun bank karta raqami.
  • Xabarlar va izohlar: ilova ichidagi chat xabarlari, sharhlar va baholar.
  • Joylashuv: faqat siz xaritada joy tanlash yoki “joriy joylashuv” tugmasini bosganingizda, ilova ochiq paytda aniq/taxminiy joylashuv olinadi. Fon rejimida joylashuv yig‘ilmaydi.
  • Rasmlar va fayllar: faqat siz o‘zingiz tanlagan rasm va hujjatlar (tizim Photo Picker orqali). Ilova galereyangizning qolgan qismiga kira olmaydi.
  • Qurilma va texnik ma’lumotlar: qurilma modeli, OS versiyasi, ilova versiyasi, push-bildirishnoma tokeni, xatolik va foydalanish statistikasi (Firebase Analytics).
  • Biometrik ma’lumotlar: ilovani barmoq izi/yuz orqali qulflash funksiyasi qurilmaning o‘z tizimidan foydalanadi; biometrik ma’lumotlar bizga yuborilmaydi va saqlanmaydi.

2. Ma’lumotlardan foydalanish maqsadlari

  • Akkaunt yaratish va tizimga kirishni ta’minlash;
  • bronlarni rasmiylashtirish, to‘lov havolasini yaratish va bron holatini kuzatish;
  • foydalanuvchi va hamkor o‘rtasida aloqa (chat) ni ta’minlash;
  • bron, to‘lov va yangiliklar haqida push-bildirishnomalar yuborish;
  • hamkorlarga to‘lovlarni o‘tkazish;
  • xizmat sifatini yaxshilash, xatolarni aniqlash va firibgarlikning oldini olish;
  • qonunchilik talablarini bajarish.

Biz shaxsiy ma’lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama maqsadida uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz.

3. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging) — statistika va push-bildirishnomalar. Google maxfiylik siyosati.
  • Yandex MapKit va OpenStreetMap — xarita ko‘rsatish. Yandex siyosati.
  • To‘lov tizimlari — to‘lov ilova ichida emas, to‘lov provayderining xavfsiz sahifasida amalga oshiriladi; biz mijozning karta ma’lumotlarini saqlamaymiz.
  • Bronni qabul qiluvchi hamkor — bronni bajarish uchun ismingiz va telefon raqamingiz shu mulk egasiga ko‘rsatiladi.

4. Saqlash va himoya

Ma’lumotlar mazzajoy.uz serverlarida saqlanadi va HTTPS (TLS) orqali shifrlangan holda uzatiladi. Kirish ma’lumotlari faqat sizning qurilmangizda ilovaning yopiq xotirasida saqlanadi. Ma’lumotlar akkaunt faol bo‘lgan muddat davomida saqlanadi; akkaunt o‘chirilgandan so‘ng 30 kun ichida o‘chiriladi, qonun bo‘yicha saqlanishi shart bo‘lgan moliyaviy yozuvlar bundan mustasno (O‘zbekiston qonunchiligida belgilangan muddatgacha).

5. Sizning huquqlaringiz

Siz o‘z ma’lumotlaringizni ko‘rish, profil bo‘limida tahrirlash, bildirishnomalarni o‘chirish va akkauntingizni butunlay o‘chirishni so‘rash huquqiga egasiz. Akkauntni ilova ichida Profil → Akkauntni o‘chirish orqali yoki ushbu sahifa orqali o‘chirishingiz mumkin.

6. Bolalar

Ilova 18 yoshdan kichik shaxslarga mo‘ljallanmagan va biz ulardan ataylab ma’lumot yig‘maymiz.

7. O‘zgarishlar

Siyosat yangilanishi mumkin; yangi tahrir shu sahifada e’lon qilinadi va sanasi yangilanadi.

Privacy Policy

Mazza mobile app · Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how “Mazza Trips” LLC (TIN 312912170, 122 Yuqori Markayuz St., Asaka district, Andijan region, Uzbekistan) collects, uses, stores and protects personal data of users of the Mazza mobile app and the mazzajoy.uz service.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: phone number (for SMS login), name, optionally email and profile photo.
  • Booking data: selected property, dates, check-in/check-out times, number of guests, payment status.
  • Host (partner) data: listing details, photos, address and geolocation, contract documents, and bank card number for payouts.
  • Messages and reviews: in-app chat messages, reviews and ratings.
  • Location: precise/approximate location only while the app is open and only when you pick a place on the map or tap “current location”. No background location.
  • Photos and files: only the images and documents you select via the system Photo Picker.
  • Device and usage data: device model, OS and app version, push token, crash and usage analytics (Firebase Analytics).
  • Biometrics: the optional app lock uses your device’s built-in biometric system; biometric data never leaves your device.

2. How we use data

To provide login and accounts, process bookings and payment links, enable chat between guests and hosts, send booking and service notifications, pay hosts, improve the service, prevent fraud and comply with law. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

3. Third parties

Google Firebase (analytics, push notifications), Yandex MapKit / OpenStreetMap (maps), payment providers (payments happen on the provider’s secure page; we do not store guest card data), and the host of the property you book (receives your name and phone number to fulfil the booking).

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on mazzajoy.uz servers and encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Login tokens are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after account deletion, except financial records we must keep under Uzbek law.

5. Your rights

You can view and edit your data in the profile, turn off notifications, and delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or via this page.

6. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.
