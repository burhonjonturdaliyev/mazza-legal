Mazza akkauntini o‘chirish

Ilova: Mazza · Ishlab chiquvchi: “Mazza Trips” MCHJ

1-usul: ilova ichida

  1. Mazza ilovasini oching va akkauntingizga kiring.
  2. Profil bo‘limiga o‘ting.
  3. Pastdagi “Akkauntni o‘chirish” tugmasini bosing va tasdiqlang.

2-usul: email orqali

Ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan telefon raqamingizni yozib, so‘rov yuboring. So‘rov 7 ish kuni ichida bajariladi.

So‘rov yuborish

Nima o‘chiriladi

  • Profil: ism, telefon raqami, email, profil rasmi;
  • chat xabarlari, izoh va baholar;
  • saqlangan joylar va push-bildirishnoma tokeni;
  • hamkorlar uchun: e’lonlar, rasmlar va bank karta ma’lumotlari.

Nima saqlanib qoladi

To‘lov va bron bo‘yicha moliyaviy yozuvlar O‘zbekiston qonunchiligi talab qilgan muddatgacha (odatda 5 yilgacha) saqlanadi, so‘ng o‘chiriladi. Boshqa barcha ma’lumotlar 30 kun ichida butunlay o‘chiriladi.

Delete your Mazza account

App: Mazza · Developer: “Mazza Trips” LLC

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Mazza and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap “Delete account” and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Email mukhtarov3991@gmail.com with the phone number of your account. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What is deleted

Profile (name, phone, email, photo), chat messages, reviews, saved places, push token; for hosts also listings, photos and bank card details.

What is retained

Payment and booking financial records are kept for the period required by Uzbek law (typically up to 5 years) and then deleted. All other data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
